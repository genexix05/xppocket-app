How to play Pokémon Essentials on iPhone

XP Pocket is an iOS player for RPG Maker games and Pokémon Essentials–based projects. You bring the game folder or archive; the app does not download or bundle titles.

  1. Get your game files

    You need a copy of the Essentials / RPG Maker XP project you have rights to (game folder or archive). XP Pocket does not offer downloads of fan games or copyrighted assets.

  2. Install XP Pocket

    When it is on the App Store, install it on an iPhone or iPad running iOS 26 or later. You can play without an account.

  3. Import the project

    Open the library and tap add. Pick the game folder or archive from Files, iCloud Drive, or another location. The title appears in your on‑device library.

  4. Play with touch controls

    Launch the game and tweak the touch layout if needed. Saves stay on device; cloud save is optional with Sign in with Apple and Pro.

FAQ

Does XP Pocket include Pokémon Essentials?

No. It is a player: it runs projects you import. We do not ship Essentials or fan games.

Which engines / RGSS are supported?

The mkxp‑z engine covers RPG Maker XP, VX, and VX Ace (RGSS). Many Essentials games target XP.

Does it work on iPad?

Yes. Same app on iPhone and iPad; import and play on‑device.
