Step by step

Touch controls in XP Pocket

PC fan games do not ship with an on-screen gamepad. XP Pocket adds touch layouts you can tailor per game and, optionally, extend with community packs.

  1. Open an imported game

    Controls appear over the game. Pause or open layout settings from the app UI as needed.

  2. Tune the layout to the title

    Move, resize, or hide buttons to match the genre (adventure, turn-based combat, etc.). Save a per-game profile if you like.

  3. Try community layouts

    The app can list packs from the public API. No account is required to browse; they are overlays, not games.

  4. Play full screen

    Once the layout fits, keep playing on‑device. The goal is for Essentials and RPG Maker to feel natural on iPhone and iPad.

FAQ

Do I pay for layouts?

Playing and importing do not require an account. Some Pro features (e.g. cloud save) are optional.

Do layouts include games?

No. Only button maps / overlays. You import the games yourself.

Does it work on iPad?

Yes. You can use the larger screen on iPad.
