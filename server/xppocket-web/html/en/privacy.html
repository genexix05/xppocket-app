Privacy Policy

Last updated: 24 August 2026

Controller

XP Pocket (xppocket.app). Contact: support@xppocket.app.

Summary

You can use XP Pocket without an account. If you Sign in with Apple or use cloud save (Pro), some data is sent to our servers to provide that service. We do not sell personal data or show ads in the app.

On-device data

  • Preferences (settings, touch layouts).
  • Games and save files you import or create while playing.
  • File metadata used by iOS (e.g. timestamps).
  • Optional local diagnostic logs if you enable them in Settings.

Optional account (Sign in with Apple)

If you choose to sign in:

  • We receive an Apple account identifier.
  • We may receive an email address (real or Apple’s Hide My Email), depending on your choice.
  • We use this to authenticate you and attach account features (e.g. Pro, cloud save).

Cloud save (Pro, optional)

If you upload or download saves, the save pack and game metadata (title, size, date) are stored on our servers linked to your account. You control when to upload or delete. We do not use your saves for advertising or share them with third parties.

Community layouts

The app may contact api.xppocket.app to list or download layouts. No account is required for browsing. Admin publishing stores the content you submit.

What we do not do

  • No third-party analytics or advertising SDKs in the current version.
  • No cross-app tracking.
  • We do not sell personal data.

Third parties

XP Pocket includes open-source libraries (mkxp-z, SDL, and others). Apple may receive anonymous crash reports if you enable them on iOS. Sign in with Apple is operated by Apple; their policy applies to that auth flow.

Retention & rights (EU)

We process account and cloud-save data to provide the service you request. We keep account and saves while your account exists or until you ask for deletion. Contact support@xppocket.app for access, correction, or deletion.

Children

We do not knowingly collect children’s data. Use follows App Store age ratings.

Changes

We will post updates on this page with a new date.
